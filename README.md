# chatgrove-sites

**60+ real landing pages for local service businesses — built as part of SMB Forge's outreach process.**

Each directory is a custom-built landing page for a real barbershop, hair salon, or beauty business that was pitched during SMB Forge's cold outreach. These are production sales assets, not demos or templates.

## What this is

SMB Forge helps service businesses automate their back office (booking, intake, SMS, follow-ups). As part of the sales process, we build a custom preview site for each prospect — showing them what their online presence could look like with modern design, their actual photos, and their business info.

- Static HTML/CSS — single-file pages per business

- Each page is unique: real business names, real photos, real service menus

## Context

These were generated during SMB Forge's outbound sales pipeline to demonstrate value before the prospect ever booked a call. The goal was to convert cold leads into warm demos by showing rather than telling.
